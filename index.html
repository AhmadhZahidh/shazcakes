How to Order

Ordering your dream cake or surprise gift is smooth and personalized. Follow these three simple steps:

01

Share Your Idea

Tell us about your occasion, desired theme, preferred flavours, cake size, or gift items. You can send reference photos or pick directly from our creations gallery.

02

Confirm the Details

We will discuss the price, design elements, schedule availability, and delivery or collection arrangements. Everything is transparent and tailored to your budget.

03

Celebrate Your Moment

Confirm your booking and relax! We lovingly bake and prepare your creation so it arrives ready to amaze your guests and celebrate your unforgettable moment.

Please note: Sending a WhatsApp enquiry does not constitute a finalized booking. Final availability, schedule reservation, and price agreements are confirmed directly between you and Shaz Cakes & Surprises.
